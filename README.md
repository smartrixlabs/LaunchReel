## ✨ One tap does the whole edit

Tap **Make it for me** and LaunchReel builds the promo for you: it picks the framing, adds a hook
headline, syncs the cuts and zooms to the music, writes captions from your voice and finishes with
an end card. Don't like it? One **Undo** puts everything back exactly as it was.

Everything it does, you can also do by hand — nothing is locked away.

## 🛠️ What's in the editor

| | |
|---|---|
| 📱 **Record your screen** | A floating bubble records any app. It hides itself while recording, counts you in 3‑2‑1, and stops when you shake the phone. Your voice is recorded too, so you can talk over what you're showing. |
| 🖼️ **Phone frames & scenes** | Drop your recording into a phone or browser mock-up, on gradients, patterns or your own picture. |
| 🎥 **Motion that follows the action** | Zooms, pans and taps that land on the moment they matter. LaunchReel can find the busy parts of your recording for you. |
| 💬 **Captions in your language** | Your speech becomes captions on the phone itself, then translates into other languages. Export one video per language in one go. |
| 🎵 **Music that fits** | A built-in, commercially licensed music library, with cuts and punches landing on the beat. |
| 🎙️ **Voiceover & clean-up** | Record narration, clean up the sound, and cut the dead air where nothing happens. |
| ⚡ **Speed ramps** | Slow down the good bit, speed through the boring bit. |
| 🔤 **Text & cards** | Hook cards, end cards and headlines with styles that already look right. |
| 🖼️ **Thumbnails** | Pull a thumbnail out of your video for the store or YouTube. |

## 📥 Install

1. Download the APK: **[latest release](https://github.com/smartrixlabs/LaunchReel/releases/latest)**
2. Open it on your Android phone (Android 8.0 or newer) and allow installing from your browser or
   file manager when asked.
3. Open LaunchReel and tap **Record screen** or **Import recording**.

> This build is signed with a development key, so Android shows the usual "unknown app" warning
> for sideloaded apps. Installing it over an older LaunchReel keeps your projects.

---

## 🔒 Privacy

LaunchReel edits and exports on your phone. Your recordings stay on your device — there is no
account, no upload and no cloud render. Captions and translation run on the phone too, so the
editor keeps working with no signal.
